# Reverie

Reverie adds a creative dreamscape to survival Minecraft.

Sleep in a Dreamweaver's Bed and wake up in a bright, open reflection of the same place in your world. Build, fly, experiment, and plan without using commands or changing your survival save. When you wake up, you return to where you fell asleep with your original inventory restored.

Dream Anchors turn parts of the Reverie into lasting project spaces with their own creative inventories. Figment Cages let you bring mobs into controlled areas when a build needs more than an empty canvas.

The goal is simple: give survival players room to imagine freely without turning the survival world into Creative mode.

## Highlights

- A creative dimension aligned with your Overworld coordinates
- Safe separation between waking and dream inventories
- Persistent project inventories through Dream Anchors
- Shared beds for multiplayer dreaming
- Figment Cages for controlled mob testing
- Curios and Accessories support, including cosmetic slots
- Server controls for time limits, sharing, mobs, and restricted content
- Recovery tools designed to protect hard-earned items

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.x
- Installed on both the server and connecting clients

Reverie 0.1.0 is an alpha release. Back up important worlds before installing or updating.

## Documentation

Read the [Reverie Wiki](https://github.com/The-RedGear/Reverie/wiki) for the player guide, configuration, commands, and recovery help.

## Building from source

Use Java 21 and run `./gradlew build` or `gradlew.bat build`. The finished JAR is written to `build/libs`.
