# Reverie

**Build without limits. Awaken without bringing the dream home.**

Reverie adds a location-aligned creative dreamscape to survival Minecraft. Sleep in a **Dreamweaver's Bed** and enter a bright white reflection of the same Overworld coordinates, where you can fly and plan builds in Creative mode. Wake up through the bed—or fall into the void—and Reverie returns you to your waking body with your original survival state restored.

## A creative canvas tied to your world

- Matching X/Z coordinates keep every dream project connected to its intended Overworld location.
- The Reverie is a bright, superflat white canvas designed for planning and experimentation.
- Dream Anchors connect nearby Overworld beds to persistent, project-specific creative inventories.
- Guests may share beds, with configurable capacity and entry costs.
- Atmospheric particles, sounds, bed animations, and pearled anchor shimmer make transitions readable in multiplayer.

## Built to protect survival progression

- Waking inventories are escrowed and backed up before the transition.
- XP, hunger exploitation, potion carryover, advancements, portals, player damage, and explosion griefing are blocked.
- A server-managed block and item blacklist rejects dangerous content regardless of how it is placed.
- Incremental loaded-chunk audits can purge illegal blocks and items without scanning unloaded terrain.
- Admin recovery and forced-awakening commands provide a failsafe when something goes wrong.

## Figment Cages

Figment Cages define chunk-based spaces where imagined mobs may exist. Charge a cage to expand its range through five visual states, set a configurable population cap, and use the in-world boundary display to see where figments are allowed. By default, only the Ender Dragon and Wither are forbidden.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft **1.21.1**
- NeoForge **21.1.0 or newer within the 1.21.1 line**
- Optional runtime support for **Accessories** and **Curios**, including cosmetic slots
- Protection for Create's direct schematic placement and Schematicannon

Reverie must be installed on both the server and connecting clients. Optional compatibility mods are not required.

## Alpha notice

Reverie 0.1.0 is an early testing release. Back up worlds before installation and test compatibility before deploying it to a long-running survival server.
