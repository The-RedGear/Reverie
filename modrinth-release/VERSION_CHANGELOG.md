Reverie 0.1.0 introduces the complete initial dream loop: enter through the Dreamweaver's Bed, build at matching coordinates in Creative mode, and awaken with the original survival state restored.

Release polish includes three custom advancements, clearer rate-limited rejection messages, startup configuration validation, `/reverie doctor`, and versioned backward-tolerant saved data.
